# Prospect Research

Sales prospect research combining contact enrichment with high-level company intel. A Cowork skill that prioritizes actionable contact data (email, phone) over lengthy reports.

## Overview

This skill finds contact info and high-level company intelligence for sales prospecting. It accepts company names, website URLs, executive names, or LinkedIn URLs as input and delivers structured research results.

## Priority Data

- **Contact Info:** Name, Email, Phone, Past Companies
- **Company Info:** Marketing Spend, Number of Producers

## Available Search Tools

- **Exa Search** — AI-powered semantic search for finding people and contact pages
- **Perplexity Search** — AI-synthesized answers for quick facts and company overviews
- **Apollo API** — Contact enrichment and employment history verification
- **Web Fetch** — Direct website scraping for contact and team pages

## Usage

Place `SKILL.md` in your Cowork skills directory and invoke when researching prospects.
